## Key Roles - Detailed Requirements

### CTO / VP Engineering
- **Must Have:** 5+ years in iGaming, experience launching a B2C platform, regulatory compliance knowledge
- **Nice to Have:** GLI/BMM certification experience, multi-jurisdiction launches
- **Key Responsibilities:** Technology vision, vendor management (game providers, PSPs), architecture decisions, regulatory tech compliance

### Compliance Officer / MLRO
- **Must Have:** ICA or ACAMS certification, 3+ years in gambling compliance, knowledge of AML5/6 directives
- **Nice to Have:** Multi-jurisdiction experience, regulatory relationship network
- **Key Responsibilities:** License applications, ongoing compliance, SAR filing, staff training, regulatory reporting
- **Regulatory Requirement:** Named on license application. Background checks required.

### Lead Platform Engineer
- **Must Have:** Experience with distributed systems, event-driven architecture, payment systems, 7+ years engineering
- **Nice to Have:** iGaming platform experience, real-time bidding or trading systems background
- **Key Responsibilities:** Platform architecture, wallet system design, API design for game providers, performance optimization

### Security Engineer (Post-Launch)
- **Must Have:** CISSP or equivalent, experience with PCI DSS, penetration testing, SOC 2
- **Nice to Have:** iGaming security audit experience, OWASP expertise
- **Key Responsibilities:** Security architecture, incident response, compliance audits, vulnerability management

## Hiring Sources for iGaming Talent

### Specialized Job Boards
- iGaming Business Jobs (igamingbusiness.com/jobs)
- Pentasia (pentasia.com) - iGaming recruitment specialist
- GamblingJobs.com
- LinkedIn (filter by iGaming companies)

### Target Companies for Talent
- Tier 1 Operators: Flutter/FanDuel, Entain, bet365, DraftKings, Kindred
- Tier 1 Suppliers: Evolution, Pragmatic Play, Playtech, NetEnt/Red Tiger
- Platform Providers: Kambi, SBTech, GiG, EveryMatrix

### Key Hiring Considerations
1. **Background Checks:** All employees require enhanced background checks for licensing
2. **Non-Compete:** Many iGaming professionals have 6-12 month non-competes
3. **Remote Work:** iGaming talent is globally distributed (Malta, Gibraltar, Isle of Man, Tallinn, Stockholm)
4. **Regulatory Training:** Budget for annual compliance training for all staff
5. **Visa/Work Permits:** If hiring in Malta or Gibraltar, factor in relocation costs

## Contractor vs. Full-Time Decision Framework

| Function | Recommendation | Rationale |
|----------|---------------|-----------|
| Core platform engineering | Full-time | IP protection, long-term commitment |
| Compliance / MLRO | Full-time | Regulatory requirement, must be named on license |
| DevOps / SRE | Full-time | 24/7 responsibility, security access |
| Mobile development | Contract initially | Can start with PWA, native app later |
| QA / Testing | Mix | Core QA full-time, load testing can be contract |
| Customer support | Outsource initially | Scale with demand, bring in-house at 10K+ players |
| Marketing | Mix | Head of Marketing FT, agencies for campaigns |
| Legal | External counsel | Specialized iGaming law firms (e.g., Harris Hagan, Wiggin) |
| Game integration | Contract | Initial integration, then hand off to full-time team |
| Data / ML | Contract to start | Build team after launch when data volume justifies it |
