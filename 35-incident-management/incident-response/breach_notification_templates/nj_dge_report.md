## Section 7 — New Jersey Player Notification

Under the NJ Identity Theft Prevention Act, written notice to affected NJ residents is
required within **30 days** of discovering and confirming the breach.

| Item | Status |
|---|---|
| NJ players to be notified | [Number] |
| Notification method | [Email / First-class mail / Substitute notice] |
| Planned notification date | [Date — must be within 30 days of confirmed breach] |
| Attorney General notification | [Filed / Planned — date] |
| Credit reporting agencies notified? | [Required if >1,000 NJ residents affected — Yes/No] |

**Notice content will include** (per N.J.S.A. 56:8-163):
- Description of the incident
- Type of personal information involved
- General actions taken to protect individuals from further breaches
- Toll-free number to contact the business
- Advice to remain vigilant and review account statements
